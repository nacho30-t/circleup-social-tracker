HOW HIGHLIFE WORKS

A simple model for social growth.

The app turns everyday social behaviour into something you can see, reflect on and build over time.

01

Highlight interactions

Each day has four slots. Add a stroke only when a meaningful interaction actually happened.

02

Build your direct circle

Add friends by email and follow their progress. Their circles help you see social momentum beyond your own.

03

Reveal the iceberg

Your direct friends are only the visible layer. Friends of friends represent the wider network beneath the surface.

04

Look back, not just forward

Previous months stay accessible so you can notice how habits, friendships and network reach change over time.

THE IDEA

Why an iceberg?

The people you know directly are only the visible part of your social world. Each person carries a network of their own. Highlife makes that hidden potential visible without turning it into a score or competition.
